MSc Project in Ergonomics · Co-supervision · EMG · Force Estimation

Musculoskeletal Symptoms, Work Postures and Hand Forces Among Sonographers

Affiliation: MSc dissertation in ergonomics (project management and student mentorship)

A comprehensive investigation of musculoskeletal disorders among medical sonographers, combining questionnaires, posture assessment, EMG and force estimation. I managed the project and mentored the MSc student from the first step.

ErgonomicsWork postureEMGGrip/push forceMentorship

Project overview

Sonographers are exposed to awkward postures, repetitive movements, forceful exertion and static muscle contraction, especially in the trunk and upper limbs.

In the first phase, musculoskeletal symptoms and work postures of 29 sonographers were assessed. In the second phase, muscle activity was quantified and grip and push forces were estimated for 10 volunteer sonographers during actual and simulated scans.

My contribution

  • Managed the project and mentored the MSc student from the first step.
  • Guided the experimental design for muscle activity measurement and grip/push force estimation.

Method

  • Symptoms and posture assessment of 29 sonographers
  • EMG recording during neck and abdominal scans
  • Simulation scans with load cells to estimate grip and push forces

Results

  • Mean grip force of 18 N and mean push force of 12.01 N were estimated during left abdominal sonography.
  • Results support prioritized ergonomic interventions and can inform future design of sonography robots or ergonomic probes.
