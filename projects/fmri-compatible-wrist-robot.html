fMRI-Compatible Wrist Robot for Brain Activation Assessment

Affiliation: Research collaboration, Tehran University of Medical Sciences, Iran

An MRI-compatible wrist robot used as a clinical research tool to study brain activation and plasticity during rehabilitation exercise. I was responsible for the design, manufacturing and assembly of the robot.

MRI compatibilityNon-metallic designPneumatic actuation2-DOF wristfMRI

Project overview

Functional MRI can be used to study the effects of rehabilitation strategies on the brain, but any device used inside the scanner room must not disturb the magnetic field or the image quality.

The robot moves the wrist in two degrees of freedom, flexion/extension and ulnar/radial deviation, using two standard air cylinders inside the MRI chamber with pneumatic transmission. A stand was designed so the wrist can be moved comfortably by the robot within the MRI machine.

My contribution

  • Designed, manufactured and assembled the MRI-compatible wrist robot.
  • Ensured all parts of the robot placed in the MRI room were manufactured from non-metallic materials.
  • Designed the stand that positions the user’s wrist comfortably inside the scanner.

Design features

  • 2 DOF: flexion/extension and ulnar/radial deviation
  • Two standard air cylinders with pneumatic transmission
  • All in-room parts made of non-metallic materials
  • Separate control system and power supply unit outside the MRI room
  • Stand for positioning the wrist in the scanner

Validation

  • Pilot fMRI study on a healthy subject with robot-driven wrist movement.
  • Brain activity was shown in the contralateral primary somatosensory cortex.
  • Pilot imaging data showed no effect of the robot on image quality.
