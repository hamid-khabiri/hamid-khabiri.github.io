Robotic Physiotherapy System for Wrist and Fingers

Affiliation: Tehran University of Medical Sciences, Iran (research collaboration)

Design and fabrication of a robotic physiotherapy system for the wrist and fingers of post-stroke patients, based on clinical observation. My responsibility was the fabrication and assembly of the robot.

Rehabilitation robotics4-DOF deviceFabricationMechanical assemblyPatient testing

Project overview

Hemiplegia, muscle weakness and spasticity after stroke often affect the upper limb. Conventional wrist and finger rehabilitation is time-consuming and costly, while robotic systems can deliver repetitive, specific exercises for longer periods.

Based on information collected from patients, user needs, design considerations and technical specifications, several conceptual designs were simulated and evaluated. The optimal design was detailed, mechanical and electrical components were selected, and the system was constructed and tested with post-stroke patients.

My contribution

  • Responsible for detailed fabrication and mechanical assembly of the robot prototype.
  • Turned the detailed design into a working electromechanical device ready for patient testing.

Development process

  • Collection of patient information and user needs
  • Conceptual designs simulated and evaluated in software
  • Selection of the optimal design and detailed design
  • Selection of mechanical and electrical components
  • Fabrication and assembly of the prototype

Testing

  • The system was tested with a small group of post-stroke patients to evaluate functionality and applicability.
  • Recruited patients were in stage three of the Brunnstrom recovery criteria.
