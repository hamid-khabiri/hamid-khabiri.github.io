SinaSim Laparoscopic Surgery Simulator

Affiliation: Sina Robotics and Medical Innovators, Tehran, Iran (Mechanical Engineer, 2019 – 2020)

SinaSim combines computer hardware, a mechanical user interface and software modules that simulate the procedures and environment of laparoscopic surgery. I designed and fabricated the simulator’s mechanical structure and mechanisms.

Medical simulatorMechanism designCAD assembliesFabricationIndustry

Project overview

Training surgeons in laparoscopic skills on real equipment is expensive. SinaSim reduces training and maintenance costs while supporting efficient learning of laparoscopic surgery skills.

This was an industrial project at Sina Robotics and Medical Innovators. My scope was the mechanical side of the system; sensors, electronics and software were developed by other team members.

My contribution

  • Designed and fabricated the mechanical structure and mechanisms of the simulator.
  • Prepared mechanical calculations, CAD assemblies and manufacturing drawings.
  • Assembled, tested, troubleshot and improved mechanical subsystems through iterative prototype development.

My scope

  • Mechanical structure of the simulator
  • Mechanisms of the mechanical user interface
  • Calculations, CAD assemblies and manufacturing drawings
  • Fabrication, assembly and mechanical testing

Project impact

  • Lower cost of training surgeons compared with conventional training.
  • Supports efficient learning of laparoscopic surgery skills.
