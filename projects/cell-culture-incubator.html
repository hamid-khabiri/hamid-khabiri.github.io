Cell-Culture Incubator with Real-Time Image Monitoring

Affiliation: Noavaran Sanat Rabin (NSR), Tehran, Iran (Mechanical Designer, 2023 – 2024)

A cell-culture incubator with real-time image-monitoring capability. I designed, fabricated and assembled its mechanical system.

Mechanical designMaterial selectionProduction planningFabricationAssembly

Project overview

The incubator allows cell cultures to be monitored with images in real time while they remain in the controlled incubator environment. My scope was the mechanical system of the device.

At NSR I also developed mechanical components for a stereotactic head frame and performed its fabrication planning and assembly.

My contribution

  • Designed, fabricated and assembled the mechanical system of the incubator.
  • Selected materials and components and prepared production planning.
  • Developed mechanical components for a stereotactic head frame and performed fabrication planning and assembly.

My scope

  • Mechanical design of the incubator
  • Material and component selection
  • Production planning
  • Fabrication and assembly

Delivered

  • Fabricated and assembled mechanical system of a cell-culture incubator with real-time image monitoring.
  • Fabricated and assembled stereotactic head frame components.
